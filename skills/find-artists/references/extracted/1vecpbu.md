## Post body

My 2nd novel ever is live! This story means a lot to me, as it was my first success on Royal Road. While it's not always easy to write, I'm glad for what this story taught me, and thankful for everyone who supported the story on Royal Road or Patreon <3

Blurb: 

***All the Mighty Meriel has to do is kill a dragon to finally retire.***

His closest friends decide otherwise.

Meriel is sealed away by his closest confidants, driven by greed, in a grand betrayal. He is locked away for decades or centuries; he isn't quite sure how long. What he is sure of is that the burning flames of vengeance never flicker away.

He crafts, builds and practices, until one day, finally, the seal is shattered and he is set free.

Yet, he steps into a vastly changed world. An era completely different from his own, with wizards, mages, and Mage Academies that have forgotten how magic works. It doesn't help that humanity itself is beset on all sides by danger and war.

His awakening reverberates through the ether and awakens mighty and powerful beasts thought banished forever. Ancient beings of great providence. Mighty forces that threaten existence itself.

Follow Meriel, with his newly developed Dragon-Crafting spells, as he prepares for what is to come.

This time, he won't be betrayed again.
