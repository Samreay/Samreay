## Post body

Good Day, Everyone! The eight and final book of Fiends For Hire, my Criminal-Organization Builder series, is out now on Amazon & Kindle Unlimited.

This was my first ever series, and while there is plenty I'd go back and change about it if I were to start it now, I still absolutely love how it turned out. Thanks to everyone who gave it a shot! And if you haven't, now that it's complete and out fully on KU, maybe consider it for your next binge.

PS: The first book will be available for free for five days, starting tomorrow, July 31!

Link: [https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H9N3TTPC](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H9N3TTPC)

\---------------

Blurb:

With the last piece needed in hand, the Fiends For Hire are ready to enact the final phase of their project. All that's left to do is win the election that would grant them global domination. But nothing's ever that simple. With the threat of an apocalypse, and when the planet itself turns against them, they must band together against the greatest foe they've ever faced. In order to save the world, first, they have to destroy it.

\----------------

As the last in the series, is obviously mostly focuses on defeating the looming threat, but it also makes to sure to tie up loose ends. I did my damnedest to make sure every mystery was answered, every character was developed, with a concise and complete ending that really brought everything to a close.

Published by Mango Media, Cover art by SlothBeing

And I'd like to give them a special thanks to both of them. They have been great to work with for Fiends For Hire, and I am so glad I get to work with them again for Self-Summon, which will come out sometime in the nearish future.
