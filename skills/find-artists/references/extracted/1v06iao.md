Hello everyone,

The second book in The Wake of Ages series is out today. To reduce the chance of reading a spoiler from the blurb for Book 2, I'll just include the blurb for Book 1 here. I hope some people check out the series. It's epic progression fantasy with mystery. Thank you.

**Blurb Book 1:**  
A civilization vanished without a trace, its records erased and its people forgotten. All that remains are the artifacts and edifices they left behind.  
  
Now those remnants are coming alive.  
  
Adam has refrained from casting a single spell because he is determined to forge something unprecedented: a universal aura. But to succeed, he must use the most lethal and famous artifact, one tied to the very mysteries he longs to unravel.  
  
As a burgeoning war pulls in one territory after another, Adam’s pursuit of knowledge drives him deeper into a conflict far greater than he imagined. But as battle lines are drawn, Adam will soon see that the greatest threat to life may come from his own kingdom.  
  
If he’s to stop it, he must take a path no one has taken before, but he won't be alone.
