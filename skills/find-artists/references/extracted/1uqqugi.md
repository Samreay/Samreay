## Post body

The day is upon us! Another installment in the adventures of our intrepid everyman is here. :)

The System came to Earth, it mutated half the people and animals, it woke up latent bloodlines, and it gave everyone access to magic. Unfortunately, most of the "mutations" were monsters, and things got pretty hectic, pretty fast.

Andy's doing okay, though, as far as things go. He's helped to carve a bit of safety out of the Sonoran Desert, turning his former trailer park into a burgeoning community with defensive boons, and an expanding cave system beneath it. Unfortunately, there's no rest for the weary, and he and his companions have plenty of exploring, defending, and leveling to do. I mean, if they hope to survive the challenges coming their way...

I think the story is a lot of fun; I know I'm not reinventing the wheel here, but I think if you like post-apocalyptic settings, and a magic-based system, you'll find a lot to enjoy. I hope you'll give it a try; now's a good time to get involved as I just wrapped up Book 3 and have at least two more on the way.

Check out the [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/Andy-in-the-Apocalypse-2-Audiobook/B0H5RH8CML) version performed by JS Arquin, or, if you want to beam the story into your eyes, you can find it on [Kindle and KU](https://www.amazon.com/Andy-Apocalypse-2-LitRPG-Adventure-ebook/dp/B0GSH7HTST), too.

Cover art was done by a human employed by Aethon.

Thanks for all the support, dear readers! :)
