## Post body

So, for Book 2 of Arcane Galaxy, Troy Osgood and I thought we’d ramp up the crazy a little…

A disastrous Dungeon run complete with undead centaurs? Check?

A Bond villain monologue so long the villain doesn’t survive it? Check.

A psycho Dwarven Mafia Heiress with a pink MurderMech? Double check. Leni has pink gems braided into her beard and drives an eight-foot Titanforge Arcanum Obliterator called Rosepetal. It was called Your Death Has Arrived before she stole it off her dad and repainted it strawberry.

Throw in a bank job that goes wrong before it even starts, a decoy mech built out of gelatin, shower leavings and about half a melon, and a Big Bad  (Leni’s dad, obviously) who lives on an enormous spherical space station that is (checks in Aethon Books lawyers) NOT a Death Star and we have ourselves a ball game.

After all, there’s already one ominous orb of doom in this book and two would just be greedy.

Swashbuckler. Out now.
