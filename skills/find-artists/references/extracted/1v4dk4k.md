Hey everyone!

Book 3 of the Ethersmith has just been released! It's a runesmithing and dungeon hunting series, and this entry has goddamned magical cannons in it. Thanks to everyone who has supported making this dream come true!

Book 3 Blurb:

**Vivi built a life in Shivenar. Now she must defend it.**

Rich, respected by demons, and finally reunited with Grandpa Thom, Vivi has everything she ever wanted. Only for the worst storm season in history to arrive, threatening to wipe the fifth level entirely off the map.

With catastrophic storms spawning unstoppable behemoths and godslayers, traditional runeswords won't be enough. Vivi must push inside-carving to terrifying new heights just to survive the onslaught.

But the abyss isn't the only threat. Ether hunters continue to challenge the safety of Vivi’s friends. Perhaps it’s finally time to confront the corrupt system that branded her a criminal.

**If the hunters want a war, the world’s greatest runesmith is ready to forge one.**
