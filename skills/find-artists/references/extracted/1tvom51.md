Hey everyone!



My newest LitRPG/Progression Fantasy book, Apocalypse Slayer, is now live on Amazon and Kindle Unlimited.



It’s the beginning of a whole new series and I’m so excited to share it with you all. The book is a LitRPG/Progression Fantasy story drawing inspiration from Korean web novels, shonen battle manga, and Japanese role-playing games. Specific inspirations include Battle Royale, Btoom!, and Monster Hunter World.



The new series features lots of spear-fighting, crafting, and skill-stealing (though not introduced until chapter 19!). 



It feels really cool to be working on a new series and applying everything I’ve learned over the course of writing Arcane Kingdom Online, Tower Climber, and Second Chance Swordsman to a new opening adventure.



I hope you check it out! Link and blurb below:



Link: [https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H3CFCLGR](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H3CFCLGR)

Rob was having a bad day even before the Integration struck.



A bloodthirsty monster appeared in his living room.



His brother—who should be dead—was suddenly back.

That all happened before the Tutorial.



Now thrust into a brutal new world, Rob is learning survival isn’t enough—power is everything.

If he wants answers—if he wants his brother back—Rob won’t just have to survive.



**He’ll have to take everything.**
