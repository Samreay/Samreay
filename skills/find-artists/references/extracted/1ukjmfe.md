***Hello everyone! The Runemaster Book 3: A Magical Academy Progression Fantasy is now out on Amazon and Kindle Unlimited!***

I'm here for the release of another book! Book 3 of the Runemaster series is now out on Amazon and Kindle Unlimited!

Blurb: Leo has found his footing at the Creator's Academy. He has a Master, an unlikely team of students, and a name that's starting to mean something. But comfort never lasts long in a school built to forge the greatest talents across the Planar System.

Now the real test begins.

The first-years are sent to prove themselves in one of the famous Trials of the Creator's Academy. Every genius in the year will be watched, measured, and pushed past the limits they thought they had. Alliances will hold or shatter. Rivalries will be settled. And the Masters of the academy are all watching to see who is truly worth their time.

Leo has come a long way from the boy who trained under his mother's roof. He has new Runes, new strength, and a bloodline that sets him apart from every other student. But talent alone has never been enough here, and the further Leo climbs, the more the academy asks of him.

And he's ready to show them a glimpse of his real potential.

***Follow Leo as he masters new Runes, deepens friendships, and steps into his legacy in this progression fantasy series, featuring an overpowered MC, a unique rune-based magic system, expansive worldbuilding across thousands of worlds, and everything else you love about LitRPG and progression fantasy.***
