Gonna do my best impression of a car salesman (I've never bought a car) here, so bear with me.

1890 followers, 3 months of posting, almost 200k words posted!

It's got everything you'd want: progression, litRPG (where the mechanics are core to the story), a non-snarky System, and an MC with a pure mage build.

Between you and me, without getting too deep into spoilers, here's some of what happens.

\-Magical brain surgery.

\-Nerd math on what a "unit" of mana is.

\-A 3v1 where our MC cheats and looks up his opponents' abilities mid-fight.

\-The MC discovers the elite knew about the apocalypse *years* before everyone else, and they had not been idle.

With glowing review titles such as "only complaint is that there isn't more," "Yes.," "This author haunts me," and 19 others, you don't have to trust just my word for it! 

But I know, I know. You only have so much time in a week, and there are so many stories vying for your attention. 

*How about I sweeten the plot?*

The story is called "*The First and Last Emperor.*" There are epigraphs that tell you our MC becomes a mythical figure after the System descends and changes Earth forever, but the question is: how?

Solis at first doesn't seem like someone who would strive for social power. In fact, he seems to despise all authority, but the more we learn about him and his past, the more pieces fit into place. 

"The Emperor inevtiably emerges," as one reader put it.

**It's on Royal Road,** nearing the end of its first major arc: [https://www.royalroad.com/fiction/153903/the-first-and-last-emperor-litrpg-sysapoc-mage](https://www.royalroad.com/fiction/153903/the-first-and-last-emperor-litrpg-sysapoc-mage)

>**This story is about:** The First and Last Emperor's dramatic rise to power in a world warned about the incoming System Apocalypse ahead of time. >**Book One: Crowned in Stone and Bone**  
Solis only ever wanted to build a life for himself and those he loves, yet every time he reaches for something out of his grasp, it's yanked away. Still, he strives for more. Separated from his loved ones, hemmed in from all sides by monsters on a deadly mountain, Solis fights to gain the power to become truly free.
