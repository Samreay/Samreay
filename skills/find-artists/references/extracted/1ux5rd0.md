For those of who haven't tried it yet, Sacrifice Mage is about Ross, a PA-turned-cultist making the most of the new world he's been summoned to with the aid of his ability to sacrifice anything he owns for power-ups.

Here's the blurb for book 3:

**Forget Ring Three, Ross is now a Gold-ranked lord of Ring Two. But a final hurdle shows itself before Ross can reach the peak of Zairgon.**

A growing cult needs to boost its coffers, and Ross's new Aspects, Attributes, and Mana Cores means he's got just what he needs to set up a legitimate business.

But Zairgon won't let a cultist climb any higher so easily. Competitors sabotage his products and clients. The nobles are making a last-ditch effort to take over his temple. And even underground gangs have a bone to pick with the shooting star of Zairgon, Ross Moreland.

All of them are nothing but opportunities for growth. Ross must overcome every hurdle, break through to Opal-ranked, and gain his Icon. His enemies are not just within the city he calls home, and as the secret of Nether Vein is finally revealed, Ross must be strong enough to take it as his own.
