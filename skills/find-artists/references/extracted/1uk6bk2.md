## Post body

Hello! I’m here to promote book 2 of *Symbiotic Ascension*! This one is titled *Symbiotic Paradox*.   
**Glenn never asked to be dragged into this cursed world. But if he can’t go home, he’ll settle for vengeance.**  
Baron Howard, the man who murdered the only people who showed him kindness, has earned a place on Glenn’s list. To reach him, Glenn needs power far beyond what any human should wield.  
His journey will lead him through ruins and divine intrigues that test his strength and sanity. With Sahro, the Black Heir swordsman, and the enigmatic Diamanes within him, Glenn pushes his limits, mastering astral spells and ascending to the Higher Circles of Magic.  
He’s done being powerless. And when his reckoning comes, no one will stand in his way.
