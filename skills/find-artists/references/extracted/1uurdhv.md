**Build a base. Hunt Treasure. Profit.**

Weapons, potions, snacks…adventurers make the best customers. Behind every successful quest, there’s a merchant fueling the dream. Sera’s ready to be that merchant.

It’s time to grow her market stall into an institution—a big-city shop ready to supply rogues and sorcerers alike. Equipped with fresh new classes, the crew gets to work, haggling, importing, and running fabulous ad campaigns in local taverns.

But as the coin rolls in, rumors swirl. There’s treasure in the Cactus Forest. Tunnels under the city need to be restored. A noble in the Obsidian Valley guards a relic from the “other world”.

If Sera wants to earn her place as a royal merchant, she’ll have to dream big.

She’ll have to build her brand, one quest at a time.
