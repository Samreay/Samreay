Just over two years ago, I started writing *The Years of Apocalypse* on RoyalRoad because I like time loops, but I couldn’t find one that did a bunch of things that I wanted to see in a time loop. A bunch of authors on RR gave me a bunch of great advice on self-promotion, which I then proceeded to ignore. Now, I need to maintain a *force shield* spell because I’ve been repeatedly blown away by the number of people recommending the story here. So first, thanks to all the folks who have done that! I still have no idea what to do about all the people saying nice things about my book.

Now, TYOA’s book 1 is leaving RR and moving to Amazon. For those folks who want a polished copy (I have corrected \*so many\* typos) with a snazzy cover, or have been waiting for the audio… it’s here! The amazing Jessica Threet has done the narration.

Ebook: [https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GHZQXZ9M](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GHZQXZ9M)

Audiobook: [https://www.audible.com/pd/The-Years-of-Apocalypse-Audiobook/B0H37B8PZJ](https://www.audible.com/pd/The-Years-of-Apocalypse-Audiobook/B0H37B8PZJ)

Cover artist is Mario Teodosio

If you’ve already read it on RR, feel free to give it an honest review on Amazon. If you already have KU and want to support the story, even just downloading it helps!

If you don’t know what it’s about, here’s the blurb:

>*Become a master arcanist. Stop the apocalypse.*

>The magitech revolution has changed the world, but it hasn’t removed its dangers. Monsters still roam the wilds, and businesses and nations alike still fight over the magical resources those monsters produce. The strange Labyrinth of the Elder Gods, buried below the earth, has started to cause periodic eruptions. Those threats all seem distant to Mirian as she worries about grades studying artifice in a remote magical academy—until an allied nation betrays her country in a surprise attack and she dies.

>However, that’s just the beginning of Mirian’s journey. After dying, she finds herself back at the start of the month, with only haunting dreams of the Elder Gods that watch over the planet to guide her. Mirian is forced to grow her magical power, find allies, fight monsters, uncover a vast conspiracy, and then win an impossible battle—and that’s just if she wants to save her town.

>Month after month, she’ll relive the apocalypse until she gets things right, testing the limits of her sanity and morals. If she fails, the world she loves will be lost.
