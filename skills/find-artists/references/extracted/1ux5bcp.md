Blurb:

>The farm didn't come with a manual, and neither did falling in love, starting a family, or defeating a Demon Monarch. But Violet's figuring it out.

>Now in her third year, Violet has her hands full planting legendary crops, hatching mythical animals, brewing potions, and uncovering the long-buried secrets behind her System. This time, the seasons bring something even sweeter than a harvest: love in full bloom, a wedding to plan, and a little one on the way.

Saving the world is just another chore on her to-do list! Follow along as Violet unravels mysteries, takes on the World Event, and builds a family with her beloved prince.

>Book 3 in Violet's lighthearted quest to become the greatest farmer of all time in this quiet living, slice-of-life LitRPG about farming, low-stakes adventure, crafting, a hint of romance, and so much more.
