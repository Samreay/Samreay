## Post body

**Hey there!**

I launched my first LitRPG/progression fantasy story on Royal Road last month. I'm excited to have real eyes on my work for the first time. If you're looking for a LitRPG where progression might be more dangerous than it is rewarding, come check it out.

Link: [Level 9 Wizard on Royal Road](https://www.royalroad.com/fiction/173244/level-9-wizard-leveling-means-death)

Cover Artist: Paris Ioannou

**One more level will kill him, but being a hero grants experience.**

Alveron is a level 9 wizard, trying to survive a world where the system wants him to level up one last time. Each quest, each act to protect the people he loves brings him ever closer to level 10 his own demise.

There is one big problem: Alveron isn't very good at avoiding quests.

Dungeons are reawakening. Old magics are twisting reality. The world of Turial is shifting around Alveron and he'll stand as the bulwark to protect the town of Caulden while unraveling the mystery of what ascension really is and how he can save himself.

Book 1 is **\[Complete\]** and posting actively. There are currently about 90k words and 31 chapters available if you're a binge reader. It's a slow-burn mystery LitRPG/progression fantasy story that has a sizeable romance sub plot that takes its time.

Things to expect:

* Dry humor
* Tragedy
* Mystery
* Found family
* Competent MC who is still human
* Lots of Wizarding
* Numbers going up feels scary
* Posts every Tu / Thu / Sun
