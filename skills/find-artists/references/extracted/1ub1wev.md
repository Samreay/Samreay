## Post body

*Prion Core* is a progression fantasy with tropes between Isekai and System Apocalypse. Earth is only a starting point. True evil shall be vanquished with surgical precision in *Revenge of the Deathless.*

Expect themes of gruesome violence and brief depictions of great evil and injustice. Reader discretion is advised.

\- Loot, Crafting, Grinding, Gear, and Epic Battles.  
\- Unique Cultivation & Magic System.  
\- Captivating Plot, Challenges, & Obstacles.  
\- Third Person Limited.

The dystopian world he grew up in would definitely come crashing down if he had any say in the matter. His whole life he got to watch as the New Human Federation kept magic for themselves in the most brutal ways possible. As soon as he prepared to do something about the tyranny and abuse of a system on the brink of collapse, it happened.

Everyone thought they had already survived the magical apocalypse due to how such magic came to Earth, but what came next was truly the end. What will the soldiers do when their railguns fail to fire and the planet faces another extinction event when they have kept the populace weak, mortal, and magicless?

The rules and requirements of magic are unknown to most, and he must struggle to learn as fast as he can while everything takes a dark turn for the worst around him. He saves who he can while growing his power and preparing for the final acts of justice he has sworn to deliver.

Enjoy satisfying power growth within an epic universe full of aliens, strange magic, and true villainy across a multitude of fantasy worlds and planes!
