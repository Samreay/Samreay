## Post body

Cover art by Duy Phan.

Some of you may remember that I shared *Messiah of Steel* here when Book 1 first launched. Since then, the series has come a long way, and with Book 3 coming out soon, I felt it was time to give Book 1 a cover that better represented the story.

It’s about a scientist in a high-tech power armor who becomes stranded in a world ruled by magic, religion, monsters, and prophecy.

Instead of learning spells like everyone else, Derek keeps upgrading his armor by studying and exploiting the same mysterious energy spheres that power the world’s magic system. His approach is simple: if magic exists, then it has rules. And if it has rules, they can be understood, tested, broken, and weaponized.

Unfortunately for him, the locals quickly become convinced he might be the prophesied messiah sent to save them.

He is not happy about that.

If you enjoy:

• magitek progression and armor upgrades  
• science-vs-magic problem solving  
• large-scale fantasy battles  
• a rational, stubborn protagonist in a faith-driven world  
• reluctant messiah tension  
• LitRPG / Progression Fantasy with a sci-fantasy edge

then the series might be your kind of thing.

Book 1 is available on Kindle Unlimited, with Book 3 launching on July 7.

You can check it out here:

[https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GMD5DTH6](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GMD5DTH6)

I’m really happy with how he captured the tone of the story.

Thanks for reading, and thanks to this community as well. I’ve discovered a lot of great Progression Fantasy and LitRPG here.
