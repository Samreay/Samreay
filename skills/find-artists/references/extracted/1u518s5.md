## Post body

After two years on Royal Road, I'm happy to announce that Dual Wielding is finally coming to Amazon and Kindle Unlimited with book 1: Ash & Ember.

This has been a long time coming, and I'm super excited to finally bring it to new readers. Huge thanks to everybody that has supported the series so far, and I look forward to getting book 2 out later this year!

Unfortunately, no eta on audible yet, but I'll be working to get that out as soon as I can as well.

I'd like to thank the very talented [Aes Munandi](https://aesmunandi.framer.website/covers) for the awesome cover art.

Well, that's all! For any of you that decide to give it a shot, I truly hope you enjoy!

Amazon Blurb:

>**“It was a respected position. They’d make plenty of money, retire earlier than most, live a comfortable life, and eventually die.”**

>Stuck in a small, idyllic valley in the middle of nowhere, best friends Corrin and Wyn have a comfortable job guarding their town’s dungeon. They have friends, family, and an easy life ahead.

>It’s just not the life they’d dreamed of.

>As children, they swore they’d become spirit knights—legendary heroes who keep order and peace across the continent of Aeora—but as years passed, that promise became nothing more than a memory. That is, until Corrin reignites that once-burning desire, and resolves to seek out the dream they’d once shared, even if he has to do it alone.

>But when a dungeon expedition goes horribly wrong, he finds himself alone and trapped in unmapped depths, hunted by monsters far beyond anything he’s prepared for.

>As Corrin struggles through the darkness below, Wyn desperately tries to reach him from above. But if he wants to save his friend, he’ll have to confront his own past, his fears, and what it is he really wants.
