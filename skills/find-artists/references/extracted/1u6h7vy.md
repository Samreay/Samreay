The first book in my series, REBELLION OF THE DREGS, is available today in paperback, Kindle, and to borrow on Kindle Unlimited. After the blurb below, I'll say a bit more.

[https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H4XB6S9R](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H4XB6S9R)

**A laborer on an industrial slave world gains lethal power from a mysterious, cosmic game. Bad time to be his boss…**

Engineered in a lab to serve the Exarch’s brutal whims, Rhone spends his life scrubbing the bottom of a tower-sized vat. The best end he and his crew can hope for is a fatal slip or a terminal infection from irradiated sludge. Then something impossible happens...

A witch from another reality etches Rhone’s soul with an arcane game system, granting him the power to wield and upgrade cursed weapons of a lost age. Now he will use his growing power to help his tribe rebel against his former rulers, but he will also become a target for rival players who want his head for a trophy.

\*\*\*

Here are some additional details for you:

The system in this LitRPG is largely inspired by my love for Elden Ring. I really like the idea that a play style can completely change depending on which weapon or item a character uses. And, I like the idea that progressing one attribute doesn't necessarily give you access to every weapon or spell. Encountering something new and cool often means that you have to rethink how you are approaching your build.

While the system leans toward the Soulsborne side, the world is inspired by Warhammer 40K hive worlds and the anime BLAME! (at least at first). We begin inside a vast industrial mega-structure.

Characters and character relationships are very important to me as a reader, so in addition to progressing in power, the main character also makes decisions (good ones and bad ones) based on his personal ties. Here's a quote from a review that made me smile:

"The characters feel very *real*. The kinda guys you know too well to be polite around. Rhone himself is a solid lead. He's smart but not smug about it. His friends round out the group naturally, and the banter between them reads like people who've known each other long enough to be brutal and loyal in the same breath."

If this seems like it's up your alley, take a look! Thank you!
