## Post body

Hey folks! [Book 6 of Ends of Magic: Ascendant](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H98Q55HL) is out, and that's the series finished! 

I'm very proud of this one. It was a tough finale to write, but I think I nailed it. Bringing permanent consequences to a society of immortals is a hard thing to do, and that's what Nathan sets out to do in this book.

At 776 pages, it's also the longest book of the series. Here's the blurb: 

***The Questors rule Davrar.***

*Immortal, all-powerful, and ancient, they decide the laws of the world itself. For generations, they've treated kingdoms, wars, and even the legendary Endings as little more than pieces in a game.*

*Nathan intends to change that.*

*Together with the Heirs, he embarks on a series of impossible quests to earn the respect of Davrar's architects. They must challenge the Questors at their own game, toppling empires, healing blighted continents, and surviving a war fought between rival Questors—a conflict where defeat is temporary for everyone except them.*

*Yet these trials are only the beginning.*

*At the upcoming Conclave, the Questors will gather to decide the future of Davrar. There, Nathan must accomplish what no army, empire, or hero has ever managed: convince a society of immortals to change.*

***As an antimage, Nathan has spent years defying the rules of Davrar. Now he intends to rewrite them.***

The Questors' immortality is pretty much the core of the whole book, and I really hope you enjoy how I focus on it. It's very hard to beat people who can't lose — but Davrar rewards risk, and the Heirs start this book weaker than an average Questor and finish it matched against the strongest.
