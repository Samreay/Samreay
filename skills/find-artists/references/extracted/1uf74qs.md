## Post body

Cover Artist: Lizbé Coetzee (@bookcoversbylizbe)

I just started posting Book 2 of [The Spellwright's Guild](https://www.royalroad.com/fiction/160303/the-spellwrights-guild-cozy-litrpg-book-1-complete) on Royal Road and wanted to let people here know that the first book is complete and ready to read! I was 100% not prepared for posting on RR, but it went really well and I can't complain. Seeing people comment on/review my work and follow along has been surreal, thanks to everyone who's checked it out! :)

Give it a try if you're interested in:

\- Wholesome vibes

\- Detailed magic/worldbuilding

\- Enchanting, brewing potions, crafting, slow-burn Guild progression

\- Learning about the System as the MC is

\- Mysterious mysteries

\- 400 pages to read

\- An exam at the end to see if you've been paying attention

\- No AI

Thanks for reading!
