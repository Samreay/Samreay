**The capital is a city of endless opportunity—and hidden rot.**

With the destruction of the Water Dragon Sect behind him, Red finally has a chance to focus on what matters most: advancing his cultivation. With access to rare ingredients and his growing mastery of Arcane Scripture, he may finally have the resources to push his strength even further.

Unfortunately for Red, his talents haven’t gone unnoticed. As unseen eyes begin tracking his every move, Red quickly learns that the capital hides far deadlier dangers than he’s faced before.

When an assassin strikes and a shadowy conspiracy begins to unfold, Red is drawn into a plot that threatens to plunge the entire city into chaos.

**And to Red, who has more secrets than allies, even the smallest misstep can mean death.**
