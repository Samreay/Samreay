## Post body

You love seeing the numbers go up. You want a protagonist who is completely ruthless. And you’d rather see the world get wrecked by a mysterious cabal your main character is running, instead of one they're victimized by. If that sounds like you, then these three books are exactly what you've been looking for.

Book 1:

After burning in a place disturbingly similar to what most would call Hell, one man is given another chance.  
The new world he finds himself reincarnated in is called Fiendfell, a land ruled by a mysterious System, a land where kings fight their own wars, where godlike beings walk among mortals, and where mysterious structures known as Dungeons appear across the land without explanation.  
But here is the thing. Our protagonist is not reborn as a man. He awakens as a Dungeon Core, the sentient heart of one of these things called Dungeons. Placed in that strange circumstance and tasked with succeeding in the mission he was given, he must upgrade his domain by spawning monsters to hunt intruders, designing deadly corridors to trap reckless adventurers, and investing in powerful guardians to stand as his domain's final line of defense.  
**Follow the adventures of our Dungeon Core protagonist, whose journey promises to be anything but lonesome.**

**Link:** [**https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GH32YQB8**](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GH32YQB8)

Book 2:

We continue our journey where we left off. After Dungeon Master 07 requested that the council release more of their kind to help reclaim the Goddess’s lost authorities, new Dungeon Masters were created and sent to reincarnate in Fiendfell.

This led to the birth of Dungeon Master 08, the protagonist of this sequel, or rather her seventh incarnation, who happens to be the daughter of the formerly royal Weiß family, a family she shared a bitter past with in a previous incarnation.

With new objectives and the same shared goal of unsealing their Goddess in mind, follow Dungeon Master 08 as she takes on a new adventure in Fiendfell.

**Link:** [**https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0F38NKN5J**](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0F38NKN5J)

Book 3:

"I cannot claim to know much about her personally," Dungeon Master 08 said,"but as a Dungeon Master, I do know this: she was the first Dungeon Master to reincarnate in a female vessel. Her reputation precedes her, with tales of her atypical personality circulating among us. However, as a human and adventurer, most of what I learned came from the legends about her. She was one of the earliest adventurers, and the only one ever said to be capable of exterminating dungeons."  
The stories tell of her time as a queen, ruling over vast lands from Ironhaven to Eastbourne, from Middlesbrough to Dalhurstover. Yet despite her throne, she wandered the wilds of Fiendfell atop her giant red wyvern, free from the conventional burdens of royalty.  
Upon learning of the demise of a fellow Dungeon Master after he found an authority in Quel'Thalas—the land of the elves—and tried to recover it, Dungeon Master 07 entrusted me, Dungeon Master 05, also known as Licht, the Lonely Light of Fiendfell, with a mission. I was tasked with contacting the only Dungeon Master who could safely retrieve that authority from its current wielder.  
To the world, she is known by many names: Queen, Kingslayer, Kinslayer. But to me, Licht the adventurer, she is Arianna. And to me, Dungeon Master 05, she is Dungeon Master 08—the strongest and oldest of all Dungeon Masters.

**Link:** [**https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H66M9FT6**](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H66M9FT6)
