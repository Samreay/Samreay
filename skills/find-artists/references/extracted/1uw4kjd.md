Demon Hat book 2 is live! Link here: [Reborn as a Demon Hat](https://www.amazon.com/Reborn-Demon-Hat-Monster-Evolution-ebook/dp/B0GSS2GRZS/ref=tmm_kin_swatch_0)

I could tell you that *Reborn as a Demon Hat* is about a sentient hat that takes over people’s minds, becomes increasingly powerful, leads a party of monsters, and generally makes the numbers go up.

All of that is true. But it only sells the premise.

So I’m not going to pretend this book is for everyone. I’m just going to tell you what kind of story it really is:

**Reborn as a Demon Hat is** ***Spec Ops: The Line*** **and** ***Apocalypse Now*** **meets LitRPG.**

It begins with an absurd premise. Then it follows that premise somewhere progressively darker, stranger, and more morally tense.

If that combination means something to you, this series was written for you.
