### Comment os4urts (parent t1_os4sp4o)

That’s actually something called “Inking”. I started out as a comic book penciler and inker. In comic art you pencil first, then ink over your pencils and ink shadows in (your darks or blacks). You also ink whatever you want to pop. 

The dark black spots give it pop. It’s not used as much in manga art, so you won’t see it unless you read stuff like Batman or Spider-Man. But yeah, in American comic art we have typical shadow gradients handled by the colorist, and heavy black spots handled by the inker. When I told the ai to color it, I asked it to keep my darks. It automatically picked up on my American drawing/inking style and only applied color gradients to the shadows I didn’t ink dark. Does that help?
