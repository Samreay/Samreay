## Post body

*He died, then aliens brought him back to life to do their dirty work. It just so happens that his new cubicle is a sun-killing starship and his work site is a land of evil dragons.*



I’ve been eating, breathing, and bleeding this story for almost a year now. I’m all the way into the second book on [Royal Road](https://www.royalroad.com/fiction/134853/this-machine-slays-dragons) (and the third on Patreon), but the first book is something I can now touch with my hands. I’ll never get over this feeling.



Anyway, I just wanted to share this happy moment with people I think would appreciate it.



If the book sounds like your jam, you can get it in all its formats on Amazon: [https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H7TDCJNX](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H7TDCJNX)
