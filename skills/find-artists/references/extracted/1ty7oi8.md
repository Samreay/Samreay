Story:

The story has a more classic feel to it, and unlike most it isn't an isekai or a system-apocalypse setup—it's told from the point of view of someone born into a world of magic.

Unfortunately, that world has been under assault by the malevolent god Khaos, who has already consumed many of the gods and turned most of the planet into Voidlands that corrupt both body and mind and must be cleansed before they can be lived in again. Only a few civilizations still hold out behind their wards, striking out or defending against the seemingly never-ending tide of traitors and the corrupted.

Book 1 is a bit slower paced, introducing the world and the characters as they grow up and make their first forays into the Voidlands.

Book 2 is faster paced, as another war kicks off and they set out to reclaim a nearby petty kingdom and cleanse it of the rot that has taken hold there.

Book 3 is halfway done, and for spoiler reasons I won't elaborate on its contents =)
