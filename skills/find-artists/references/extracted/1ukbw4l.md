It is with great pleasure that I come here today to let you guys know that Book One of The Villain Noble's Guide is now out on Amazon and Kindle.

However, before any of that, see more of the story below:



[Amazon Link](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H3Z3LD7Q)



Blurb: **I died and reincarnated as the Devil.**

Well… not the actual Devil, but something close enough: **Count Valen Ashmoor.**

In the novel I read years ago, Valen was a political monster. A noble with a large private army. A reputation made in manipulation. And so many enemies that it would be easier to count his allies.

Now I'm him.

Worse? All my fiancées want me dead. Romantic, right?

To survive, I'll need to navigate court politics, dodge assassins, and somehow figure out the plot to this unfinished story.

The original Valen was cruel, manipulative, and feared.

I am… not prepared.

**New Goals:**

Don't get murdered.

Don't act like a Villain.

Don't let my fiancées figure out I have no idea what I'm doing.



In this novel's world, villains don't get redemption arcs. **We get graves.**

**And I really, really don't feel like dying twice.**
