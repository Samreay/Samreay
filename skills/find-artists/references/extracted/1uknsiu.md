Howdy Folks! I'm proud to announce the launch of my debut novel! Ebook only at the moment, but we all have to start somewhere! Someday there will be an audiobook, someday...  
  
anyways, here's the blurb for book one:

**A reluctant hero. A dangerous power. And a System already waiting for him to fail.**  
  
Finn Walsh woke up hungover in a fantasy world. No memory of how he got there, a sarcastic British voice in his head calling him a disappointment, and apparently a quest to track down a beast of legend, maybe find a way home. Sounds simple enough, right?  
  
Unlike most of the heroes in the books Finn hasn’t read, he has no background in role-playing games, no understanding of how classes or skills work, and no clue who to ask. So, when Finn finds himself in a tutorial from the moment of waking, he’s way out of his depth.  
  
As if that wasn’t enough, Finn is required to pick a starting class from a short list if he wants to get any information at all. Without even knowing what the classes are.  
  
Finn chose Mage… for reasons.  
  
It doesn’t take long for him to discover that the magic feels incredible.  
  
And that’s a problem.
