Hi everyone, 

Harmon Cooper here aka (I won't list all my nicknames) with a brand new release!

**Shadowborn Exile 2 is out from Podium with narration from Raphael Corkhill and cover art by Mario Teodosio.** 

I'm very excited for you all to read this one if you haven't already, mostly because I'm finishing up the third and final book now so I'm at that emotional - this is it - moment.

But really - dark progression fantasy about an outcast shadow warrior whose home is destroyed by a child made of light that he... ends up adopting. Think things like Wolf and Cub or videogames like Pragmata. Sacrifice, found family, progression, intense battle scenes and a beautiful landscape (a world spiraling downward) for it all to play out in.

**Check it out, KU, ebook, print, audio.** 

Thanks!

The third and final should be up for preorder shortly and out in December.
