## Post body

The fifth book in my Cardsmith series has just been released on Amazon and Kindle Unlimited:

[https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B0H98H13NW](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B0H98H13NW)

\-----

Irwin has achieved what few ever do. Now, as a newly forged heartcarded smith of emerald rank, it is time for him to take the next step of his journey.  
  
Time, however, is running out, and his home world of Giard will soon be nothing but shattered fragments floating in the primordial chaos. Away from the safety of Scour, and with barely a plan, he meets someone who shouldn't be alive. Someone who points him in the direction he must go. Now with a mission, and alongside his closest friends and a crew of outcasts, Irwin must command a ship through the vastness of the Portal Gallery into the unknown, dealing with deadly assassins, void-pirates, and terrifying monsters.  
  
Their goal? Find a hidden safe haven for a world where everyone might already be dead...  
  
And if they aren't dead? Who will even recognize the weak, sickly boy Irwin once was, now that he has become a towering giant?  
  
\-----

For those new to the series, it's a weak-to-strong, card-based story featuring forging and music.

\-----  
Cover by the awesome Ben Moran - go follow him on Deviantart!

Also, a little added news: audiobook 2 is out, and audiobook 3 should be released in a few weeks!
