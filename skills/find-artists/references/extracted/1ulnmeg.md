Academy politics will be Reivyn’s biggest challenge yet. 

Reeling from the knowledge of his parentage and desperate to become powerful enough to chart his own path, Reivyn journeys to where all ambitious noble scions go: the Magic Academy.

Dungeon diving and honing his myriad mana types is nothing compared to navigating the treacherous waters of the aristocracy. Reivyn must succeed at both. The path to the higher kingdoms doesn’t open for everyone, and with competition nipping at his heels, Reivyn needs to take risks to win. 

A fact made complicated when Reivyn senses another using his own forbidden power. To keep his own secrets and protect the Academy from the threat, he sets out to investigate. 

But walking the razor’s edge inevitably leads to a fall. In the depths of a dungeon where no powerful saviors can intervene, Reivyn’s future is far from secured. 

A locked door. A foreign prince. A mystery *no one* is ready to be revealed.
