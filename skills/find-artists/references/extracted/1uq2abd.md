It's been a full year since I first launched [The Shrouded Peaks](https://www.reddit.com/r/ProgressionFantasy/comments/1uq221k/its_my_book_birthday/), and to celebrate, I've dropped the price to $0.99 through Sunday, 7/12. Come give it a look!

Blurb:

**One young man begins his journey. His path will forge a legend to shake both heaven and earth...**

When a chance encounter with a powerful immortal opens the door to his dreams, He Yu jumps at the opportunity to become a cultivator. But when he arrives at the famed Shrouded Peaks Sect, he quickly learns the true life of a cultivator isn’t anything like in the stories. The outer sect is a world of ruthless ambition and cutthroat competition, and He Yu only has three short months to earn his permanent spot. Even worse, he’s the weakest disciple by far, and has to start from practically nothing.

Armed with an ancient cultivation art, and guided by a powerful mentor, He Yu will brave bandits, demonic cultivators, and worse… all for the chance to carve his name upon the world. The inner sect awaits. All He Yu has to do is beat the top talents of the outer sect in the annual tournament—including his rival from back home. Easy, right?

But while the outer disciples bicker with one another, a long-forgotten threat stirs. And nobody is prepared for its return.
