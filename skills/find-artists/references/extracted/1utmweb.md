## Post body

I'd like to say that it has been smooth sailing for me, but it really hasn't been. The journey was rocky and stressful, though that didn't come without its upsides. The account termination and pre-order cancellation set back book 2 release by months. At the same time, it allowed me to reach out to a publisher for an audiobook and get both book 1 and book 2 properly edited.

And now, the second ebook and first audiobook of Ceaseless Horizons are live on Amazon and Audible! The launch for the former is going amazingly as it stands. Thank you for all the support! It means a lot to me. 

**Book 2 Blurb:** 

***Lev narrowly survived a disaster. It was merely the first of many.***

*The Final Cavern deepened Lev's suspicions about his gift of reincarnation. And just like him, the Exalted have also realized his inevitable fate. Instead of wallowing in despair, both of them prepare to fight his destiny.*

*On his journey, Lev meets an unlikely mentor. Unconvinced about his potential, the man probes Lev's class and talents, only to be left dumbfounded. Lev's power rises sharply with every improvement—meteorically so when he challenges Windkeep's dungeons.*

*But threats lurk in every corner, even the well-lit ones. Darkness tails his every step, and the world refuses to leave him alone. Yet his desire to test himself against his generation's best burns bright, and a tournament approaches to allow just that.*

*Armed with a ridiculous class, he journeys to humanity's greatest bastion. There, he discovers truths that could shake the foundations of the world, as well as people he might be able to call friends one day. And maybe, just maybe, his past as an Earthling isn't as insignificant as it seems.*
