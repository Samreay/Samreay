## Post body

Aest Belequa here again, this time announcing Stormblade: Volume Three on Kindle! Book Three’s a fun one. I enjoyed writing it a ton, and the first two books’ reception on Kindle has been phenomenal. Becoming a full-time, professional author has been a dream of mine for a long time, and Stormblade is starting to make that dream more of a reality, so thank you all!

As for audio, Royal Guard, Aaron Camacho, and Onyx Volcan are currently cooking it up. More news when I know more! 

Link: [Stormblade: Volume Three on Kindle](https://www.amazon.com/Stormblade-Three-Portal-Apocalypse-LitRPG-ebook/dp/B0H23FTNPT/) 

***Kade must reforge his core.***

Kade’s victory over the Paragon of the Hurricane has cost him dearly, but his weakened state is temporary. The God of Thunder has a method to rebuild his core—a necessary step along his Path. Meanwhile, part of the expedition to Carlsbad has stayed behind to quell the S-Rank portal in the caverns. While one of Kade’s enemies steps in to fill the power vacuum, an old rival finds himself running out of time and in need of assistance.

Meanwhile, Ellen’s conflict with her father, the wealthy head of the Traynor Corporation, reaches a boiling point, and Jessie’s got her heart set on a guild building—a *pricey* one. As Kade rebuilds his core and pushes to catch up with Ellen, his eye is focused on home.

It’s not the only one.

Monstrous powers are on the move, and Earth is their focus. When the Carlsbad expedition is defeated, Phoenix finds itself under siege and outmatched. To shore up its defenses, the Governing Council organizes a tournament for the city’s up-and-coming delvers. The prize? Entry into a unique portal world. Kade must claim that world’s power.
