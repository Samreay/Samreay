## Post body

Link: [https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H444K9FK/](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H444K9FK/)

After accidentally becoming a sorcerers apprentice at a failing governmental institution, Pim Fudworth finds out that in government, having Common Sense is a superpower. 

Especially when your familiar is an incubus of ambition.

Set in a Food-Themed Fantasy City’s Poorest District, we follow Pim and his demonic familiar, Brenaris, as they solve magical complaints. 

How? 

With Common Sense, kindness, and a dash of magic. 

Or in Brenaris’ case, ruthlessness, tricks, and more style than should be cramped into any incubus of his style. 

Watch this odd pairing as they help people with their talking cookbooks, ghost-infested mugs, and doors with personality issues. 



In King’s Eat, the quests are complaints, the fight is against bureaucracy, and the reward is making peoples’ lives better.



If you want to laugh, feel useful, and believe that kindness can change things, read Common Sense!



And for the audiophiles, SoundBooth Theatre are signed on to bring Pim and Brenaris 4 book journey to life!



Thanks so much to my co-author Shawn Wilson for this. As I’m sure you guys can imagine, writing with him is a pleasure and a privilege!



As for the traditional comparison, we've drawn a lot of inspiration from Discworld, Legends and Lattes, The Office, and Parks and Recreation :)
