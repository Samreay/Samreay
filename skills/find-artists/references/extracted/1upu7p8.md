# Lost Mask - Available Today!

First and Foremost is my new book, Lost Mask. It is available now!  
Amazon: [https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H3JJVLC7](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H3JJVLC7)

Kirantha Lost was born into a world where an ancient war ruptured the ley lines, shattered the world tree, and forever changed the world and civilization. Now the only way for people to use magic safely is by refining and absorbing magical cores from monsters. Kira was found as a baby in the snow and raised in an isolated village where she is the only nonhuman. Kira doesn’t know what she is, just that she’s different and barely tolerated. After completing her coming of age trial, her core is found to be deviant, and she is banished. Given only a vague direction to another city, Kira must fight her way through monster-infested wilderness and hope to find a safe haven before winter descends. Can she survive on her own, and what type of reception will she receive should she make it out of the wilderness?

**Lost Mask is a Slow-Burn, Slice of Life Progression Fantasy, with elements of crafting, monster hunting, and LGBQT+ relationships. Spice Level is Low. (For Now!)**

# Elite Born - Available Today

Second we have the Audiobook of my debut Novel dropping, Elite Born. You can find it Here!  
Audible: [https://www.audible.com/pd/B0H365LCK1](https://www.audible.com/pd/B0H365LCK1)  
Amazon: [https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B0DBJ6CKQK](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B0DBJ6CKQK)

Aurora has always been different. She was marked from birth as an Elite and born with an additional skill that allows her, a phoenix, to take human form as naturally as breathing. Now, after five years of living with a cult that worships her kind, learning the local tongue and culture, it's time to spread her wings and venture out where few of her kind have been before. Filled with tales of the city, the lands beyond, and other fantastical creatures, she yearns to see it all and start a life unlike any other phoenix. But Aurora must remain cautious, for phoenix feathers are powerful, rare ingredients, even from one as young as her. The young phoenix must blend in, lest she end up in a cage.

**Elite Born is a slow-burn, slice of life story with elements of LitRPG and F/F romance included.**

# Reborn Elite - Preorder Live! Available 08/11/26!!!

This is the sequel to my debut Novel. The audiobook is dropping today!!! You can find it here!  
Audible: [https://www.audible.com/pd/Reborn-Elite-Audiobook/B0H7HPXT6Y](https://www.audible.com/pd/Reborn-Elite-Audiobook/B0H7HPXT6Y)  
Amazon: [https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DWP5HWP6](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DWP5HWP6)

After two years, Aurora has returned. While the phoenix finds herself within the familiar lands of the Glimmering Sands, she is far from any place she recognizes and must deal with the consequences of what happened. Aurora quickly encounters a group of people running from the oppressive culture of one of the desert's many city-states, looking to start a new life. Can she help them find their place in the world? Will she overcome her issues and return to Sahket to face what she missed over the past two years?

**Reborn Elite is a slow-burn, slice of life story with elements of LitRPG and F/F romance included.**
