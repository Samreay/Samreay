## Post body

Hello everyone!

I'm mostly a lurker in this subreddit, but you all have been pretty insightful on the genre and I wanted to let you all know that I finally managed to launch the second book of my Progression Fantasy/Epic Fantasy series, Hedge Knight, titled A Trial of Six Virtues.

The book is available for paperback, eBook, and audiobook (which is narrated by me, the author!). In addition, the eBook is on sale for $0.99 until 7/20/2026.

The first book of the series, The Knight from Nothing, is available for free as well until 7/14/2026. This is also available in paperback and audiobook.

That gives you two book for the price of $0.99!

If you're looking to jump into something new, now is the perfect time to do so!

The series is available here: [https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DZZZ9ZZ5](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DZZZ9ZZ5)

Here is the description for Book Two:

**Secrets lie beneath the Golden Peak.**

With the ruthless Band of Red ousted, the village of Goldshire recovers from their violent occupation. Helbram and his party aid in rebuilding, but restlessness persists among the people. An ancient door long sealed since time immemorial has been unearthed within the depths of the mountain: a door that, if mishandled, will obliterate the entire region and all who inhabit it.

But hope yet remains in a chance encounter.

Scholars from far and wide have come to investigate the mysterious Golden Peak and venture into its depths. However, their zealous inquiries couldn't prepare them for the dangers that await.

**And ancient trials long forgotten begin again.**

**---**

And for those just jumping in, the description for Book One:

**Fortune favors the bold. For Helbram? He’ll have to make due with relentlessness instead.**

A lifetime of denial, combined with a condition that has left him without the spiritual powers of the Awoken or the magical might of Mages, has cracked and scuffed an unbreaking will that continues to march along a path that seems to deny him at every step. Ambition still burns within, but it’s been reduced to embers, fueling hands that still reach for goals that remain out of reach.

Or so he thought.

A mission to wipe out a band of bandits goes wrong and Helbram is forced into a fight for survival along with the stragglers of the failed expedition, trapped by technology from an age long past. Within the chaos of this conflict, the faded flames of dead dreams spark anew.

**And the first steps of a legend make themselves known.**

**---**

The series aims to tell complete, standalone adventures with each book while maintaining a core, central cast that harkens back to the age of pulp novels and old school Sword and Sorcery, but with the tone and flow of Progression Fantasy and Epic Fantasy novels. I try to keep things focused on one area and on a single thread so I can let the character interactions shine through the most.

If you do pick it up, all I ask is that you consider leaving a rating or review after you've read it to let me know how I'm doing. I'm always receptive to feedback, and am constantly looking to know what I'm doing right and what I could improve upon.

Either way, I hope you enjoy the adventures!
