## Post body

Hi! I'm trying to prep what will be my next series. I've learned a lot so far with my current books, and a LOT about how different webnovel publishing is from traditional publishing... I intend to finish my current books, of course, but I am trying to plan, prep, and look forward to my plans for my next project.

I got on RoyalRoad's Rising Stars the first time I released my book. Then I got sick. I finished college. I dropped the ball and spent time instead rewriting and editing. That means I have slower progress now as the cost of choosing to do that. It's rough, but it gave me confidence that if I set my mind to it, I can do it.

Now, I have plans, outlines, scheduling, and so many documents to make sure that I don't run into the same issues even if I get sick again. I give a damn about my own consistency now and am determined.

With that, I am trying to pick between a few basic premises to start the backlog on and would love opinions on what people think would be more enjoyable to read. I have *many* worlds, but alas only ten fingers to type. (None of these are the titles... I'm just categorizing.)

1. "Cape shit" - A superhero world with a big hole in LA and a lot of questions that need answered. Very episodic. Very villain of the week. Has a clear end to the first big bad, and potentially could continue after that. This one is the loosest "progression" fantasy one but still has room for power growth.
2. "Cape shit but with kids" - Superhero world but an underground academy of super kids that are being engineered to be ultimate weapons for the US. Has a clear-ish end but could easily continue. Progression fantasy but a bit lower-fantasy.
3. "Tower" - LitRPG tower story with lots of progression and powerful characters. Towers appear across Earth. Climb the tower to become a god. Very dark with lots of death. Complex story that might be a bit rough to get done while working on others. Has a clear end, but hooooh boy it's a long one.
4. "Molecule magic" - A story with living gods and magic based loosely on how molecules work. My current winner in terms of where my brain is at. A romance story and progression fantasy. Needs more foundational work because the story is less 'set', but has a clear progression in my head.
   1. My biggest question with this one is which would be more interesting; romance with a god or a more mundane romance?

Thank you if anyone answers! <3
