## Post body

Hello, everyone!

After posting on RoyalRoad for two years and writing Taste of Magic series for a year, I have finally gathered the courage to self-publish my first (and hopefully not my last) book. With over 4000 followers and more than 1,5 million views this story managed to get during this time, I’m feeling cautiously optimistic.

It’ll be launching on Kindle, Kindle Unlimited, Print and even Audible! Yes, even Audible. Despite self-publishing the ebook and paperback, Soundbooth Theater have agreed to produce the audiobook for me. You know, the same guys who made the audiobook for Dungeon Crawler Carl.

While I’m definitely not deluding myself in to thinking that I’ll be the next Matt Dinniman, I still hope you’ll give either my ebook or audiobook a try. I have listened through the whole audiobook myself simply because I enjoyed the Soundbooth Theater production so much.

Main narrator is Jessica Threet and she has done an absolutely amazing job of portraying Melina. Jack Fields is supplementing the male cast, but you also might hear cameo from Jeff Hayes. Which character is he playing? Why don’t you give it a listen and try to figure it out?

Cover artist: Tsuu and Kara

AI usage: None. I can make my own mistakes

Anyway, enough rambling here’s the blurb of this cozy cooking LitRPG:  
  
**What if an all-powerful Archmage became a baker?**  
After the path of the \[Archmage\] led the world to its destruction, Melina rewound time and chose a different life. Instead of chasing power, she opens a small bakery in the sunlit town of Sunglow, hoping for a quiet routine, honest friendships, and the comfort of freshly baked sweets.  
But the past does not disappear so easily. Old allies return, long-buried enemies resurface, and with every pastry she creates, Melina uncovers more about the Church, the people she cares about, and the choices that once shaped the fate of the world.  
As she learns new recipes, builds connections, and settles into her new role as a baker, Melina begins to grow again, not as a conqueror, but through steady, satisfying progression. With the help of the System, her baking evolves into a unique form of magic, creating restorative treats that heal body and spirit alike.  
**A cozy LitRPG fantasy about second chances, slice-of-life progression, and finding comfort, community, and purpose in a small-town bakery.**
