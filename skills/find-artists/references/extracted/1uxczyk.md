## Post body

I've always loved *Lord of the Rings* and wizardposting culture, and I wanted to write a LitRPG that captured that feeling of sitting around a campfire with old wizards telling tales and smoking pipes.

It's not just a cozy adventure story, though. There's actual progression and monster fights too. It's my first book, and I'm really excited to finally publish it on Amazon. 

I worked with the artist who did the covers for the *He Who Fights with Monsters* series because I thought his style really matched the tone of my book. His name is Harry Bui, and I can't recommend working with him enough. I just love how the cover turned out.

So without further ado, here's the link and the blurb:

[https://www.amazon.com/Ponder-Orb-LitRPG-Adventure-Fantasy-ebook/dp/B0GZQM8XWR](https://www.amazon.com/Ponder-Orb-LitRPG-Adventure-Fantasy-ebook/dp/B0GZQM8XWR)

**Ponder the Orb**

When Mark is ripped from Earth and dropped into a realm of wizard towers and magic, he is taken in by an unlikely group of hermits who spend their days smoking halflings’ leaf, arguing over spell theory, and quietly pondering glowing orbs.

They call themselves the Blue Wizards.

The powerful White Tower sect calls them a disgrace.

But as Mark begins training with them, he realizes something unsettling:

Their strange “orb pondering” might actually be a lost path to real power.

As he learns to cultivate mana, unlock spells, and navigate a mysterious inner world that shouldn’t exist, Mark is pulled deeper into a system that clearly has plans for him.

Unfortunately, those plans involve overflowing rifts, undead hordes, and a rapidly escalating necromancer problem that’s beginning to threaten the entire Middle Realm.

It turns out slowing down, smoking pipeweed, and contemplating the universe is a lot more dangerous than it looks.
