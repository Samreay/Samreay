***Hello everyone! The Runemaster Book 2: A Reincarnated as a Baby Prog Fantasy is now out on Amazon and Kindle Unlimited!***

I'm more than excited to share that the second book of the Runemaster series is now out on Amazon and Kindle Unlimited! This is what you'd call nowadays a Reincarnated As a Baby book, and here's the blurb below!

*Perfect for fans of The Beginning After the End, Elydes and Bog Standard Isekai!*

***Having endured a childhood of training and lectures, Leo must now prove himself to his peers in the most famous academy across the Planar System.***

After years of training under his mother and her subjects, it is time for Leo to spread his wings and take on a greater adventure… this time under a Master known as one of the greatest Runemasters to have ever lived.

Danger lurks in the dusty halls of the Creator’s Academy, the birthplace of geniuses from great Empires and ancient kingdoms. Everyone here is a monstrous talent. There are friends to make, alliances to strike, trials to overcome, and enemies to face.

But the academy is more than a place of learning. Between demanding classes, rival students, strict Masters, and trials that push every genius to their limits, Leo must find his place in a school where talent alone is never enough.

His first life might have been full of miserable school experiences, but now, Leo is confident he can turn the tide in his second life.

***Follow Leo as he masters new Runes, makes new friends, and explores his legacy in this progression fantasy series, featuring an overpowered MC, a unique rune-based magic system, expansive worldbuilding across thousands of worlds, and everything else you love about LitRPG and progression fantasy.***
