## Post body

We’ve all been there. You tell yourself you’re just going to read one more breakthrough or fight scene, and suddenly the sun is coming up, your eyes are aching, and you’ve completely neglected your mortal responsibilities.

​If this sounds like you, it’s time to join us at Cultivators Anonymous!


​Here is our 6-step support program:

​1. Acknowledge the Problem: Scan the blurb below. If deranged cultivators, manipulating Gods, and mischievous foxes give you a sudden rush of dopamine, you are officially an addict.

​2. Begin Treatment: Start your journey with our first 21 ‘Introductory Sessions’ (Chapters) on [Royal Road](https://www.royalroad.com/fiction/169779/cultivators-anonymous/chapter/3444560/0-lkeris-and-the-fox ). Discover a fast-paced, weak to strong, progression fantasy with cultivation style power levels and comedic moments.

​3. Follow the Program: Hit that Follow button so you never miss a ‘daily treatment’ (new Chapter drop) at 17:17 BST.

​4. The Severe Case: For those with a truly uncontrollable addiction, binge up to Chapter 38 immediately on [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/collection/2179318).

​5. Group Therapy: Come chat with other addicts and Alice the cheeky fox on [Discord ](https://discord.gg/AQ3Dpv2Bdu).

​6. Achieve Ascension: Reach the peak of the Heavens and realize that recovery was never the goal. Daily Chapters and a severe reading addiction are actually the highest form of enlightenment!


​The Blurb:

​After a joke ‘ritual’ somehow succeeds, student Mira Quin and her friends are dragged into the secret side of our world.

Stuck between manipulating Gods, deranged cultivators, and mischievous foxes, Mira must figure out how to survive, master her magical crown, and take revenge. Asking out that cute boy will just have to wait!

- ​Progression: A weak-to-strong progression fantasy with cultivation-style power levels, an item-based magic system, and sci-fi elements.

- ​Tone: Fast-paced action with light-hearted comedic moments.

- ​Scope: The start of a genre-hopping epic series that will follow Mira’s journey from dungeons to superheroes, and eventually, the stars.

- ​Schedule: Daily chapters at 17:17 BST on Royal Road!
