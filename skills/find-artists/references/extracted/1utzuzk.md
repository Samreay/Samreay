## Post body

Heya everyone. Scared and tentative first-time author here.

 

 Book 1 of \*The Yielding Law\* is finished and uploading to Royal Road - first book is complete start to end, so no risk of catching up to a hiatus. There's much more planned, but I wanted a finished book in hand before asking anyone to commit their reading time.

 

 \---

 

 Corin Marsh knows the fen better than anyone in Brackwater: which channels silt after hard rain, which soft banks take a man and don't give him back, the smell the water gets a day before it ices. He is seventeen and content. He is also carrying a secret even he doesn't fully understand: a whole life already lived, folded away behind his eyes.

 

 Then the census comes.

 

 In the Greyfen Marches, a counting of souls is never just a counting. Clerks tally sons for a war that won't admit to being one, and behind the clerks rides a scion of the immortal Vael dynasty, hunting something far rarer than levy-fodder. One soul in a million wakes to power. Those who are found are claimed. Those who run are hunted.

 

 On census day, in a line of villagers waiting to be written into the ledger, something wakes in Corin.

 

 The dynasties take their power by force, and force is the only way they know. They have never met a man who has spent a lifetime learning how to yield.

 

 \---

 

 \*\*What to expect\*\*

 

 \* \*\*Cultivation in a Western-medieval world.\*\* Fen villages, tithes, censuses, and inns, all ruled from far away by immortal royal dynasties. Each royal family doubles as the sect: it hoards the techniques, and it owns every cultivator it can find.

 \* \*\*A reincarnated MC who is actually old.\*\* Corin carries sixty years of a quiet prior life. He reads rooms, keeps his head down, and plans past the current fight. No shouting about defying the heavens.

 \* \*\*An unorthodox path.\*\* Orthodox cultivation forces essence to obey. Corin can't force anything, so he yields. It turns out the rules have a hole in them the exact shape of him. Hard-magic rules that hold, and wins that come from cleverness inside them.

 \* \*\*Slow-burn, character-first progression.\*\* Daily-life texture and side characters who pull their weight, then the pace picks up: legible power, earned breakthroughs, costs on the page.

 \* \*\*Real stakes.\*\* Cultivators are one-in-a-million and act like it. Deaths matter and villains are patient. The kindest kingdom on the continent has not said what its peace costs.

 \* \*\*What you won't find:\*\* no system or stats, no harem, no instant OP protagonist, no villain-of-the-week face-slapping.
