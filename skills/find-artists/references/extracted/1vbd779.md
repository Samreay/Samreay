## Post body

Hello!

As the title says I am releasing the first of my series onto Amazon today, and the first full novel I have written. I spent the last 8 months working on this every day. For now it will be limited to ebook release until I can afford to have traditional editing and a proper cover created. Audiobook will be coming soonish. I aimed for a lot of character growth, slow burn, and dark humor. Some found family as well. I hope you like it!

Blurb:

James Harlow is straight up not having a good time. After failing to drink away the pain of losing his wife a year prior, he has earned the hate of his son, Troy, instead. Not one to back down from a little challenge, he decides to day drink himself into doing a little civic remodeling by crashing into a telephone pole.

Waking up handcuffed to a hospital bed wasn't exactly on his list of 'Ways to Enjoy a Lazy Afternoon', but not exactly a surprise. Nothing a little alien invasion can't clear up. Only, the invaders snatched his son on live television. And then they snatched James.

Now James must survive an insane game world on an alien spaceship full of magic, too-tight leather pants, and murderous woodland creatures. Sober, angry, and probably a little too mouthy, James is ready to do whatever it takes to find his son, and get off that ship. Even if it means playing nice with a space god.

Comps would be: Dungeon Crawler Carl and Discount Dan
