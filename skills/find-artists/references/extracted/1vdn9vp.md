## Post body

Hey, all. I know a few folks here have read Two-World Traders (previously Sailor's Rise) [on Royal Road](https://www.royalroad.com/fiction/110264/two-world-traders-progression-fantasy)—and I know not everyone here reads on Royal Road. To that end, with even more editing and after many cover iterations, I moved the first book to Amazon and KU yesterday (stubbing hurts me too; please blame Amazon).

It's got business building and a light but unique cultivation system of sorts, and they're ultimately connected. It's a duology, so meaty but not a lifetime commitment, you know (book two is 2/3 written).

[Universal Amazon link here.](https://www.royalroad.com/amazon/B0H9J38ZPM?maas=&ref=)

Description to save you a click:

*Elias Vice, a supposed nobody from the backwater town of Acreton, has already written his destiny, and it ends with him building the greatest business empire Sailor’s Rise has ever known.*

*But to get there, Elias will need to turn a pocketful of relics into a company worth millions. The Rise’s caste-like business and political system stratifies power and access in the dock-ringed merchant republic, and Elias is starting with neither.*

*And yet he is not the nobody he first believes, as Elias’s connection to an enigmatic people—airship travelers with a unique mastery over space itself—will both help and haunt his ambitious endeavor. Elias, it turns out, is a collector.*

***A rags-to-riches progression fantasy from the award-winning author of*** ***Trial of the Alchemist. The Two-World Traders duology will conclude with*** ***Trader's Crown, coming 2027.***
