## Post body

Hey everyone, I'm excited to announce the release of book 2 of my other series.

If you enjoy exploring magic, a slow-burn progression, and small but determined protagonists, this one might be up your alley. Book 2 is where the promised magic academy starts, and I hope you enjoy reading it.

Huge thanks to my publisher, Mango Media, for making this happen. 

Thanks again to Kart Studio for once again providing the cover art.

The Audiobook is once again narrated by the extremely talented Hannah Schooner and Giancarlo Herrera. Big thanks to Royal Guard for arranging them for my series.

Links: [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H1LD9VP1) | [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/B0H12TMCRB)

Blurb:

>Lily has accomplished her goal of being accepted into Regarth the country's premier magical institute... but now what?  
Time to learn more magic, of course!  
While the teachers at Regarth cannot compare to Arakil, the god of magic himself, there's still plenty to learn and experience. From potions and enchanting to combat. Lily is motivated to gain knowledge and catch up to her sister, Rose.  
But, Lily isn't the only one eager to learn. She makes friends with humans and demihumans alike on her journey through this world of magic.
